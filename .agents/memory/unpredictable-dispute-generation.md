---
name: Unpredictable dispute generation
description: Product preference that the next eligible prize tier should not follow a predictable ascending or descending sequence.
---

Choose the next dispute tier unpredictably from the currently eligible tiers; do not use ascending or descending prize value as the generation order. Keep financial eligibility distinct from the random ordering, so operators can inspect why each tier qualifies or is blocked without exposing the upcoming selection to users prematurely.

**Why:** The user said the next dispute should be unpredictable and a simple ascending or descending order is not the best default.

**How to apply:** Preserve a fair, auditable random selection among eligible tiers and expose eligibility criteria separately from the upcoming public selection.
