---
name: Dispute intermission
description: Product rule for the transition between a completed dispute and the next one.
---

After a dispute ends, keep its result card visible alongside the next-dispute countdown during an intermission. Do not start the next dispute immediately. This applies to both winning and no-bid results.

**Why:** The user clarified that the intermission is a required product state and both cards must remain visible together.

**How to apply:** Schedule the next dispute after the intermission, expose its absolute start deadline from the server, and render the previous result and next countdown until the new dispute becomes live.
