---
name: Initial treasury funding
description: User-confirmed initial platform contribution and meaning of tier minimum bids.
---

The platform plans an initial R$1,000 capital contribution. Treat it as planned, not available cash; record it only after the funds are actually received, separately from user top-ups and bid revenue.

For prize tiers, “lance mínimo” means the increment between bids, not an opening bid amount. Use the existing R$0.10 increment as the default until per-tier increments are configured.

**Why:** The user confirmed the R$1,000 initial contribution and selected the increment interpretation. Available treasury cash must not include expected funds.

**How to apply:** Do not seed or count the contribution before receipt is confirmed. Keep contribution entries separate from user wallet activity; apply the increment meaning when configuring or displaying prize tiers.
