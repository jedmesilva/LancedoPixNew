---
name: No-bid dispute result
description: Product rule for presenting disputes that expire without any bids.
---

When a dispute ends without bids, present it as an explicit no-bids outcome, not as a zero-value winning bid. Do not show a winner amount or winning imagery in that state.

**Why:** The user said the result must not display “Lance vencedor” with R$0,00 when nobody bid.

**How to apply:** Keep no-bid results visually and verbally distinct from actual wins, while preserving the normal winner summary when a bid wins.
